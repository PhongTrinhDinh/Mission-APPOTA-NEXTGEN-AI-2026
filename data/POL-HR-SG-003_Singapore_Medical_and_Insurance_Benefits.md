### SECTION 1: OUTPATIENT MEDICAL SUBSIDIES
1. **Panel Clinic Consultation:** Confirmed employees are entitled to cashless outpatient consultations at designated MHC/Raffles Medical panel clinics across Singapore, up to a maximum cap of **SGD 500 per calendar year**.
2. **Specialist Outpatient Consultation:** Specialist visits are reimbursable up to **SGD 1,000 per calendar year**, strictly upon written referral from a general practitioner.

### SECTION 2: WORK INJURY COMPENSATION (WICA)
1. In accordance with the Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA 2019), Appota SG maintains mandatory work injury compensation insurance covering all employees.
2. The policy indemnifies employees against occupational accidents occurring during the course of employment, including business travel and work-from-home approved hours.
3. Medical claims under WICA must be notified to HR within **24 hours** of the accident occurrence.

### SECTION 3: GROUP HOSPITALISATION AND SURGICAL (GHS) COVERAGE
1. Permanent employees are enrolled in the company's Group Hospitalisation & Surgical policy with annual claim limits up to **SGD 30,000 per disability event**.
2. Probationary employees become eligible for GHS coverage only on the first day following formal employment confirmation.
