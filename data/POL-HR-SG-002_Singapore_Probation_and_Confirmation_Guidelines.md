### SECTION 1: PROBATION PERIOD IN SINGAPORE
1. **Standard Duration:** The standard probationary period for new hires in the Singapore office is **3 months** to **6 months** depending on seniority level:
   - Junior to Mid-level positions: 3 months.
   - Senior and Management positions: 6 months.
2. **Extension of Probation:** Management reserves the right to extend the probationary period by an additional duration (up to a maximum cumulative total of 6 months) if performance evaluation requires further assessment.

### SECTION 2: LEAVE ENTITLEMENT DURING PROBATION
1. **Annual Leave:** Employees on probation do **not** accrue consumable annual leave until confirmation. However, upon successful confirmation, leave entitlement is backdated pro-rata from the initial commencement date.
2. **Medical Leave:** In line with MOM guidelines, outpatient sick leave is only claimable after completing the first 3 months of continuous employment. Prior to completing 3 months, any medical absence is treated as unpaid leave.

### SECTION 3: NOTICE PERIOD DURING PROBATION
1. Either party may terminate the employment contract during the probationary period by giving **1 week's written notice** or paying **1 week's gross salary in lieu of notice**.
2. Notice period must be served during official working days and cannot be offset by unconfirmed annual leave.

### SECTION 4: CONFIRMATION PROCESS
1. Two weeks prior to the probation end date, the People Operations team issues the Probation Review Form to the employee's line manager.
2. Confirmation is formally documented via a written **Confirmation Letter** signed by the Managing Director. An employee is only deemed confirmed upon physical or digital receipt of this letter.
