### SECTION 1: APPLICABILITY AND JURISDICTION
1. This policy strictly applies to all employees engaged by **Appota SG Pte. Ltd.** stationed in Singapore.
2. In the event of any conflict between Group-wide policies and Singapore statutory employment regulations, Singapore Employment Act (Cap. 91) statutory protections shall prevail for Singapore employees.

### SECTION 2: STATUTORY PUBLIC HOLIDAYS IN SINGAPORE (11 DAYS)
Employees in Singapore are entitled to **11 paid gazetted public holidays** per calendar year in accordance with the Ministry of Manpower (MOM):
1. New Year's Day (1 day)
2. Chinese New Year (2 days)
3. Hari Raya Puasa (1 day)
4. Good Friday (1 day)
5. Labour Day (1 day - May 1st)
6. Vesak Day (1 day)
7. Hari Raya Haji (1 day)
8. National Day (1 day - August 9th)
9. Deepavali (1 day)
10. Christmas Day (1 day - December 25th)
*If a public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is designated as a paid public holiday in lieu.*

### SECTION 3: ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT (SINGAPORE)
1. **Base Entitlement:** Confirmed full-time employees are entitled to **14 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
2. **Progression by Tenure:** For every completed consecutive year of service with Appota SG, annual leave increases by **1 additional day**, up to a maximum cap of **18 working days** per annum.
3. **Carry-over Rules:** A maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year and must be consumed before **30 June**. Any unutilized carried-over leave shall lapse without monetary encashment.

### SECTION 4: OUTPATIENT SICK LEAVE AND HOSPITALISATION LEAVE
In compliance with MOM requirements, confirmed employees who have served for at least 3 months are entitled to:
1. **Paid Outpatient Sick Leave:** Up to **14 working days** per calendar year, subject to the production of a valid Medical Certificate (MC) issued by a registered medical practitioner.
2. **Paid Hospitalisation Leave:** Up to **60 working days** per calendar year (inclusive of the 14 days outpatient sick leave) if hospitalised, quarantined, or certified by an approved medical specialist.

### SECTION 5: CENTRAL PROVIDENT FUND (CPF) CONTRIBUTIONS
1. For Singapore Citizens and Singapore Permanent Residents (SPRs), Appota SG makes statutory CPF contributions at prevailing rates determined by the CPF Board based on employee age bands.
2. Foreign employees holding Employment Passes (EP) or S-Passes are exempt from CPF; alternative private retirement or medical insurance arrangements apply as stipulated in their respective employment contracts.
